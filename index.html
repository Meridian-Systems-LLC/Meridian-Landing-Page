What we fix

Sound familiar?

These are the problems we hear about most from businesses with 10 to 75 people.

“I don't trust the numbers.”

Reports pull from your business software through a connection that can break after an update and go stale without anyone noticing. We rebuild it, watch it, and alert you when it fails.

“Only one person knows how it works.”

We document what you have, including the connections nobody wrote down, and take ownership so your business doesn't depend on one person.

“It broke after the update.”

Software and Windows updates can break reports and links between systems. We test changes first and repair what breaks, so updates stop being a risk.

“Our website and our order system don't match.”

We keep your web store, CRM or email in step with QuickBooks, Sage or SAP, so orders and stock counts agree and exceptions get flagged.

“Our insurance renewal is asking questions we can't answer.”

Insurers now expect multi-factor login, endpoint protection and tested backups, and supported computers. We check your answers against your insurer's questionnaire and close the gaps.

“The person who handled our IT left.”

We document what you have, clean it up and take it over, so there's one number to call when something breaks.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is the checkup really free?

Yes. It's a 30-minute conversation and a one-page written report. We only look and take notes; we don't change anything on your systems, and there's no obligation.

We already have an IT company. Can you still help?

Often, yes. Many IT providers don't handle the software side, like connecting QuickBooks or Sage to your other tools. We're happy to work alongside your current provider.

How does pricing work?

Projects are quoted at a fixed price after the checkup, with a written scope. Monthly support is a flat fee per user. Contact us for a quote based on your business.

Are we too small for this?

If you have around 10 or more people and rely on your computers to get work done, you're exactly who we work with.

How do we work together?

Most work starts with a phone call, then a video meeting on Microsoft Teams or Zoom. We come on site when it helps. You'll get a written scope and price before any work starts.

Which business software do you work with?

QuickBooks, Sage and SAP Business One most often, plus Microsoft 365, web stores and reporting tools like Power BI. If you run something else, ask us.

Do you work on machines and production equipment?

We handle the computers, networks and software around your equipment. We don't program machine controls; for that we work alongside your controls integrator.

Who owns our accounts and data?

You do. Your passwords, your data and your systems stay yours, and we hand everything over whenever you ask. Agreements are written in plain language and we explain them before you sign.
