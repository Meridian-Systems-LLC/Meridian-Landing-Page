What we fix

Sound familiar?

These are the problems we hear about most from growing businesses.

“I don't trust the numbers.”

Reports pull from your business software through a connection that can break after an update and go stale without anyone noticing. We rebuild it, watch it, and alert you when it fails.

“Only one person understands our integrations.”

We document what you have, including the connections nobody wrote down, and take ownership so your business doesn't depend on one person.

“It broke after the update.”

Software updates can break reports and the links between systems. We test changes first and repair what breaks, so updates stop being a risk.

“We're still re-typing the same data twice.”

We read invoices and purchase orders automatically, route them for your approval, and create the record in your system — so nobody types it in twice.

“Our data migration left things behind.”

We move customers, vendors, items, and open invoices to your new system, then reconcile the totals so you know exactly what came across and what didn't.

“Nobody can tell me why QuickBooks and our ERP don't match.”

We find where the two systems disagree, fix the connection causing it, and document it so the next discrepancy gets caught fast.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is the scoping call really free?

Yes. It's a 30-minute conversation and a one-page written scope. We only look and take notes; we don't change anything on your systems, and there's no obligation.

We already have an IT company. Can you still help?

Often, yes. Many IT providers don't handle the software side, like connecting QuickBooks or your ERP to your other tools. We're happy to work alongside your current provider.

How does pricing work?

Projects are quoted at a fixed price after the scoping call, with a written scope. Contact us for a quote based on your business.

Are we too small for this?

If you rely on your accounting or ERP software to get work done and the systems around it don't quite talk to each other, you're exactly who we work with.

How do we work together?

Most work starts with a phone call, then a video meeting on Microsoft Teams or Zoom. We come on site when it helps. You'll get a written scope and price before any work starts.

Which business software do you work with?

QuickBooks and your ERP or CRM most often, plus Microsoft 365, Azure, and reporting tools like Power BI and SQL. If you run something else, ask us.

Who owns our accounts and data?

You do. Your passwords, your data and your systems stay yours, and we hand everything over whenever you ask. We explain every agreement before you sign.
